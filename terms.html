1. Scope

These terms apply to your use of this website and to research services provided by NEXERA Metrics (“we”, “us”), founded by Om Singh and based in India. 2. What we provide

We provide e-commerce research and written reports. Our three offers are Existing Product Profit Fix, Single Product Validation and Full Launch / Expansion. Each ends in one full written report. We research and recommend; we do not run your store, listings, advertising or stock.

Research reports contain estimates and assumptions. They support decision-making but do not guarantee sales, profit or business outcomes. Results depend on your decisions, your execution and market conditions that nobody can fully predict.

Our reports are not financial, legal or tax advice. Where a report mentions GST, fees or other costs, it does so to frame the numbers, not to advise you on your tax or legal position. Please speak to a qualified professional for that.
