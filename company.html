About NEXERA Metrics

NEXERA Metrics is an India-based e-commerce research agency founded by Om Singh. We help sellers make data-backed product, sourcing, pricing and launch decisions on Amazon India, Flipkart, Meesho and other Indian marketplaces.

We offer three services: Existing Product Profit Fix, Single Product Validation and Full Launch / Expansion. All of them follow the same 7-stage research workflow and end in one full report.

We work from public data and disclosed assumptions, and we label estimates as estimates.

Founder story

NEXERA Metrics was founded by Om Singh to give Indian e-commerce sellers structured, evidence-based product research before they commit money to stock.

Too many sellers pick a product on a hunch and find out about fees, returns or supplier limits only after the stock has arrived. Om started NEXERA Metrics so that the checking happens first.

Our India-first approach

Our research is built around how selling in India actually works.

  • Festival and seasonality tracking

    We map how Indian festivals and seasons change demand, supply and pricing.

  • Marketplace fees

    Fees on Indian marketplaces are built into every margin estimate.

  • GST-aware framing

    Costs and prices are framed with GST in mind.

  • COD and RTO awareness

    We account for cash-on-delivery returns and return-to-origin losses.

  • Supplier verification

    We check price, minimums, lead times and product details.

  • Bulk review analysis

    Review-import tooling we built ourselves helps us read buyer reviews at scale.

Our 7-Stage Research Workflow

One connected flow from question to recommendation.

  1. Understand

    Understand the business and confirm decision needs.

  2. Find

    Find possible products using listings, search trends, questions and competitors.

  3. Check demand

    Check demand with search interest, activity, price history and seasonality, including the impact of Indian festivals on demand, supply and pricing.

  4. Study market

    Study the market by comparing features, prices, ratings, reviews and buyer needs.

  5. Check suppliers

    Check suppliers for price, minimums, lead times, shipping and product details.

  6. Calculate costs

    Calculate unit cost, margin and break-even from all-in costs and fees.

  7. Recommend

    First re-verify the work from the earlier stages so no assumptions slip in. Then combine everything into one full report with a clear presentation. Explain risks, state unknowns and recommend the strongest options.
