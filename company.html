About NEXERA

An India-based research and analytics agency serving e-commerce sellers around the world.

Who we are

NEXERA was founded by Om Singh to give online sellers the kind of structured product research that large brands buy from consulting firms. We combine marketplace research, review analytics and financial modelling in one engagement.

Mission

Help sellers avoid costly product mistakes by making market evidence clear and affordable.

How we work

  • Evidence first

    Every conclusion points to data we can show you.

  • Clear assumptions

    Estimates are labelled and limits are stated.

  • Confidentiality

    Your brief, products and numbers stay private.

  • Plain language

    Reports are written to be understood, not to impress.
