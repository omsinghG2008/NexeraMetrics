Plan My Launch

Our 7-Stage Research Workflow

One connected flow from question to recommendation. Each stage lists what is included and the most important part.

  1. Understand
  2. Find
  3. Check demand
  4. Study market
  5. Check suppliers
  6. Calculate costs
  7. Recommend

Understand

Understand the business and confirm decision needs.

What is included

  • The product, the marketplace and where you are today
  • The decision you need to make
  • Budget, timing and limits to work within
  • Research scope confirmed before work starts

Most important part

A clear question. Everything after this stage depends on it, so we settle what you need to decide before any research begins.

Find

Find possible products using listings, search trends, questions and competitors.

What is included

  • Marketplace listings and bestseller pages
  • Search trends and the questions buyers ask
  • Competitor ranges and new entrants
  • A shortlist of candidate products

Most important part

Looking wide first. A broad first pass makes sure a better option is not missed before the list is narrowed.

Check demand

Check demand with search interest, activity, price history and seasonality.

What is included

  • Search interest and buyer activity over time
  • Price history
  • Seasonality, including how Indian festivals shift demand, supply and pricing
  • Lasting demand separated from short spikes

Most important part

Telling real demand from a spike. A festival peak can look like growth, so this stage protects you from stocking for demand that will not last.

Study market

Study the market by comparing features, prices, ratings, reviews and buyer needs.

What is included

  • Feature, price and rating comparison
  • Bulk analysis of buyer reviews
  • What buyers want and what they complain about
  • Gaps that competitors leave open

Most important part

Reading what buyers actually say. Reviews show where competitors fall short, and that is usually where the opening is.

Check suppliers

Check suppliers for price, minimums, lead times, shipping and product details.

What is included

  • Supplier price and minimum order
  • Lead times and shipping terms
  • Product details and quality signals
  • Verification checks on the supplier

Most important part

Proving the supplier can deliver. A strong product still fails if price, quality or timing cannot be met.

Calculate costs

Calculate unit cost, margin and break-even from all-in costs and fees.

What is included

  • Landed unit cost
  • Marketplace fees and GST-aware framing
  • Shipping, packaging and advertising
  • COD and RTO return losses, then margin and break-even

Most important part

Counting every cost. Many products only look profitable until fees, returns and shipping are included, so this shows the true margin.

Recommend

Re-verify the earlier work, then combine it into one full report with a clear recommendation.

What is included

  • Re-check of every earlier stage so no assumption slips in
  • One full report with a clear presentation
  • Risks explained and unknowns stated
  • The strongest options, ranked

Most important part

A recommendation you can act on. We re-verify first, then give a clear answer with the risks and unknowns stated plainly.

How each figure is calculated

No hidden maths. These are the formulas behind the calculator above.

Calculator formulas
MeasureFormula
RevenueUnits sold × selling price
Total costsProduct + shipping + fees + ads + fixed costs
Gross profitRevenue − (product cost + shipping)
Net profitGross profit − fees − ad spend − fixed costs
Net marginNet profit ÷ revenue
ROINet profit ÷ total costs
ROASRevenue ÷ ad spend
Contribution marginPrice − variable cost per unit (product, shipping, fees)
Break-even units(Ad spend + fixed costs) ÷ contribution margin per unit
