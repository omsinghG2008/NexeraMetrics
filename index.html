Every product must survive three questions

Most failed products were chosen on instinct. We test each idea against demand, competition and profit before you commit money to stock.

  • Is the demand real?

    We separate lasting demand from short spikes, so you do not stock a fad.

    How we check: search interest, sales-rank signals and seasonality.

  • Can you win against existing sellers?

    We look for the gaps rivals leave open, in price, listings and product quality.

    How we check: listing audits, pricing and review complaints.

  • Does the maths still work after every cost?

    We model profit after product cost, shipping, fees, ads and fixed costs.

    How we check: landed cost, margin, ROI and break-even.

We show our sources, state our assumptions and label estimates as estimates. If the data cannot support a conclusion, we say so.

How a project runs

Six steps modelled on how established research firms structure an engagement: brief, scope, collect, validate, analyse, deliver.

  1. Discovery call

    We learn your goals, budget, target marketplace and the decision you must make.

    You receive: a written brief

  2. Scope and proposal

    Deliverables, method, timeline and data limits are agreed in writing before work starts.

    You receive: a scope document

  3. Data collection

    We gather public listings, prices, reviews and search-interest data for your category.

    You receive: a raw dataset

  4. Validation

    Duplicates, outliers and suspicious reviews are flagged. Sources are logged for every figure.

    You receive: a source log

  5. Analysis and modelling

    Demand, competition, sentiment and unit economics are analysed and modelled in scenarios.

    You receive: a working model

  6. Review and handover

    We walk you through the findings, answer questions and deliver the report and spreadsheet.

    You receive: a report and sheet

Frequently asked questions

What exactly does NEXERA do?

We research e-commerce products and markets and model the numbers behind them, so sellers can decide what to sell, where to source it and what price to charge.

Which marketplaces do you research?

We work from publicly available information on marketplaces and online stores such as Amazon, Etsy, eBay, Walmart, Shopify stores, Flipkart and Meesho. We have no affiliation with any of them.

Do you guarantee sales or profit?

No. We deliver evidence, estimates and clearly stated assumptions. Results depend on your execution and the market, and our reports are not financial advice.

How long does a project take?

It depends on scope. A single-product report and a multi-product comparison need different time, so we agree a timeline in the written scope before starting.

What do I receive at the end?

A written report, a spreadsheet with the data and calculations, and a walkthrough call to explain the findings.

How do you price your work?

Pricing depends on the number of products, markets and depth required. Send a brief and we reply with a quote.

How is my information protected?

Your brief and data are used only for your project and stored with access controls. Read the privacy policy for details.

Can we work together long term?

Yes. Many sellers need ongoing research, so we can set up a recurring monthly arrangement after the first project.

Tell us what you plan to sell

Share your product idea, market and budget. We reply with a written scope and next steps.
