Choosing a product on instinct is expensive

Most failed products were chosen on instinct. For sellers in India the risks stack up: marketplace fees and GST change the real margin, cash-on-delivery orders bring returns and RTO losses, and festival seasons swing demand, supply and prices.

  • Demand that looks strong but is only a short spike
  • Margins that disappear after fees, GST, shipping and returns
  • Suppliers whose price, minimum order or lead time does not hold up

Every product must survive three questions

We test each idea against demand, competition and profit before you commit money to stock.

  • Is the demand real?

    We separate lasting demand from short spikes, so you do not stock a fad.

    How we check: search interest, sales-rank signals and seasonality.

  • Can you win against existing sellers?

    We look for the gaps rivals leave open, in price, listings and product quality.

    How we check: listing audits, pricing and review complaints.

  • Does the maths still work after every cost?

    We model profit after product cost, shipping, fees, ads and fixed costs.

    How we check: landed cost, margin, ROI and break-even.

We show our sources, state our assumptions and label estimates as estimates. If the data cannot support a conclusion, we say so.

Built for Indian e-commerce

Our research is built around how selling in India actually works.

  • Festival and seasonality tracking

    We map how Indian festivals and seasons change demand, supply and pricing, so stock and launches are timed with evidence.

  • Marketplace fees

    Fees on Amazon India, Flipkart, Meesho and other Indian marketplaces are built into every margin estimate.

  • GST-aware framing

    Costs and prices are framed with GST in mind, so margins are not overstated.

  • COD and RTO awareness

    We account for cash-on-delivery orders, returns and return-to-origin (RTO) losses that quietly reduce profit.

  • Supplier verification

    We check price, minimum order, lead time, shipping and product details before a supplier reaches your shortlist.

  • Bulk review analysis

    Review-import tooling we built ourselves helps us read large volumes of buyer reviews for complaints, needs and risks.

Frequently asked questions

What does NEXERA Metrics do?

NEXERA Metrics researches e-commerce products and markets for Indian sellers and models the numbers behind them, so you can decide what to sell, where to source it and what price to charge.

Who founded NEXERA Metrics?

NEXERA Metrics was founded by Om Singh, who leads the company as its founder.

Which marketplaces do you research?

Amazon India, Flipkart, Meesho and other Indian marketplaces, using publicly available information. NEXERA Metrics is independent of all of them.

Which offer should I choose?

If a product is already live and underperforming, choose Existing Product Profit Fix. If you have one idea to decide on, choose Single Product Validation. If you are launching or expanding into a new category or marketplace, choose Full Launch / Expansion.

What do I receive at the end?

One full report with a clear presentation of the findings. It explains the risks, states what is still unknown and recommends the strongest options.

How long does it take?

Turnaround depends on the offer and on how many products and marketplaces are covered. We confirm the timing with you in writing before work starts.

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on the number of products, the marketplaces and categories covered, how deep the competitor and supplier research needs to go, and how fast you need the report.

Do you manage my store or run my ads?

No. We research, calculate and recommend. You decide what to do with the findings.

How is my information used?

We use what you send only to reply to you and deliver your project, and we ask only for the details we need. See the privacy policy for the full details.
