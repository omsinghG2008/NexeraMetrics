Existing Product Profit FixHome & kitchen

Profit review for a home-and-kitchen product

Challenge
A steady seller whose returns were rising and whose margin was getting thinner each month.
Approach
Compared competitor listings and prices, read buyer complaints in bulk, and rebuilt the margin with marketplace fees, return costs and ad spend included.
Outcome
A ranked list of fixes: clearer size and use details on the listing, one product issue to raise with the supplier, and a pricing change to test first.

Single Product ValidationFestival gifting

Launch check for a festival gifting product

Challenge
A seller wanted to launch before the festival season and needed a clear go, fix or no-go.
Approach
Mapped demand build-up around the festival, checked competitors and reviews, verified supplier lead time and minimum order, and modelled landed cost with COD returns.
Outcome
A go-with-conditions recommendation: order timing that fits the demand window and a launch price that keeps margin after every cost.

Full Launch / ExpansionMulti-category

Second-category plan for an existing seller

Challenge
A seller with one strong category wanted to add another and sell on a second marketplace.
Approach
Shortlisted candidate products, compared category demand and seasonality, checked supplier options, and modelled costs and fees for each marketplace separately.
Outcome
A prioritised launch plan: which product to start with, which to hold, the launch dates against the festival calendar, and the main risks for each.
