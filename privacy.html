4. Legal basis

Where laws such as the EU/UK GDPR apply, we rely on your consent, steps taken at your request before a contract, and our legitimate interest in running a secure business site. In India we process personal data in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Depending on where you live, you may ask to access, correct, delete or export your data, withdraw consent, or object to processing. Email us and we will respond within a reasonable time. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.
