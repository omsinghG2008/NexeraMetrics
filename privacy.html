1. Who we are

NEXERA Metrics (“we”, “us”) is an e-commerce research agency based in India, founded by Om Singh. You can reach us at nexerametrics@gmail.com. Our offices are in Agra, Trans Yamuna Colony, Uttar Pradesh 282006 and Shahdara, Janta Flats, Delhi 110093.

2. What we collect

We collect only what you choose to send us through the contact form or by email:

  • your name and email address
  • your WhatsApp number, only if you enter it (optional)
  • your product or niche, marketplace, current stage and main problem
  • a budget, only if you enter it (optional)
  • the message you write

Our hosting provider records standard technical data such as your IP address and the pages requested, as most web servers do. We do not run advertising trackers or third-party analytics on this site.

3. Why we use it

We use your details to reply to you, to scope and deliver any project you ask for, to keep a record of our conversations, and to prevent spam and abuse. We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

We rely on your consent when you submit the form, and on our need to respond to the request you made.

4. Who processes it

We use a small number of services to run the site. They process data for us and for no other purpose:

  • Web3Forms receives what you type into the contact form and passes it to our inbox.
  • Google (Gmail) hosts the inbox where we read and reply to messages.
  • Google Firebase Hosting serves this website.
  • Google Fonts supplies the fonts, which means your browser contacts Google when a page loads.

These providers may process data outside India. We do not publish client names, reports or research.

5. How long we keep it

We keep enquiries and project correspondence for 12 months after our last contact, then delete them unless the law requires us to keep them.

6. Your choices and deletion

You can ask us to show you what we hold about you, correct it, or delete it, and you can withdraw your consent at any time. Email us at nexerametrics@gmail.com from the address you used, and we will respond within 30 days.

7. Security

The site is served over HTTPS. The contact form asks for the minimum information, checks and cleans what is entered, and blocks automated spam. No online service is perfectly secure, so please do not send passwords, bank details or customers’ personal data.

8. Cookies and storage

We do not set advertising or analytics cookies. Your browser stores two small items for the site to work: a flag in session storage that remembers whether the animated background loaded, and, if you use the contact form, the times of your recent sends in local storage so we can limit repeat submissions. Neither is sent to us.

9. Children

Our services are for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Third-party links

This site links to LinkedIn, Instagram, X and our Web Division site (nexerawebs.web.app). They have their own privacy practices, which we do not control.

11. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.

12. Contact

Questions about this policy: nexerametrics@gmail.com.
